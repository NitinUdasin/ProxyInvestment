# India Alcoholic Beverages Sector — Investment Note

---

## 1. The Trend

India's alcohol market is quietly undergoing a generational shift. A young, urban, aspirational consumer base is trading up from cheap country liquor to branded whisky, craft beer, and premium spirits — and this upgrade cycle is still in its early innings. With rising incomes in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities and gradual regulatory easing across states, the structural demand story here isn't a flash in the pan — it's a decade-long runway.

---

## 2. The Proxy Logic

Picking the winning alcohol brand is a gamble — state excise rules can flip overnight, and valuations on the big names like UBL (PE: 92x) and Radico (PE: 73x) already price in a lot of optimism. The smarter play is to back the **picks-and-shovels businesses** — the glass bottle makers, packaging companies, and ethanol technology providers that profit *regardless* of which brand wins the shelf. These enablers tend to trade cheaper, grow steadily, and carry less regulatory headline risk.

## 4. Watch Out For

- **State Excise Policy Risk** — Alcohol regulation in India is a state subject, and policy whiplash (price caps, distribution changes, prohibition threats) can hit revenues overnight with zero warning. This is the single biggest wildcard in the sector.

- **Valuation Stretch on Direct Plays** — The obvious names (UBL at 92x PE, Radico at 73x PE) leave very little room for error. Any earnings disappointment or volume slowdown could mean painful corrections even if the long-term story stays intact.

- **Input Cost Volatility** — Glass, molasses, grain, and packaging materials are all commodity-linked. A spike in input costs — as seen in recent years — can rapidly squeeze margins, especially for mid-sized players without pricing power.
